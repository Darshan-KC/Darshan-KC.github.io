Requirements (implemented)

- Meta title/description
- Open Graph
- Twitter cards
- Canonical URLs
- Structured data (JSON-LD: Person, WebSite, Article, TechArticle, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, ProfilePage, ContactPage)
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- RSS feed (/rss.xml)
- Semantic single-h1 page hierarchy
